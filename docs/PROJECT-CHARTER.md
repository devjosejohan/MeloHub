# MeloHub
## Project Charter — Version 1.0

**Project owner:** Johan  
**Status:** Initiation  
**Working name:** MeloHub

### 1. Mission

MeloHub is an independent, curated digital directory dedicated primarily to music, audio technology and resources for music enthusiasts.

Its purpose is to help users discover reliable applications, useful tools, interesting projects and carefully evaluated products.

### 2. Vision

Become a trusted discovery platform for music enthusiasts and technology lovers.

Quality, transparency and simplicity will take priority over content quantity.

### 3. Philosophy

MeloHub is built around three principles:

- Reliability: published information must have identifiable sources.
- Transparency: recommendations and verification criteria must be visible.
- Simplicity: the platform must remain easy to navigate and maintain.

### 4. Main Content Areas

- Audio: music players, streaming tools, audio editors and music utilities.
- Mobile: Android applications and mobile resources.
- PC: Windows and Linux applications.
- Tools: useful software and technological resources.
- Customization: system and device customization.
- Products: headphones, IEMs, DACs, amplifiers and accessories.
- News: selected music, audio and technology news.

Content may belong to multiple categories.

### 5. Editorial Policy

Every published entry must satisfy appropriate verification requirements.

Applications require identifiable sources, documented compatibility and accurate licensing information.

Open-source claims must be supported by an identifiable license.

Product recommendations require credible reviews or documented evaluations.

News must have identifiable sources and editorial relevance.

Unverified submissions must never be published automatically.

### 6. Community

The initial catalog will be managed by the project owner.

Future trusted contributors may participate through a controlled suggestion and review process.

### 7. Distribution

MeloHub will provide links to legitimate external sources.

It will not host application installers or redistribute software without authorization.

### 8. Technical Philosophy

- Maintainable architecture.
- Structured and reusable content.
- Minimal unnecessary dependencies.
- Responsive design.
- Accessibility and performance.
- Automated testing.
- Git-based version control.
- Clear technical documentation.

### 9. Initial Success Criteria

The first public release should include:

- Responsive website.
- Organized content categories.
- Searchable catalog.
- Individual application pages.
- Reliable external links.
- Documented content structure.
- Maintainable architecture.
- Basic automated tests.

### 10. Long-Term Direction

Potential future capabilities include community contributions, editorial administration, curated product recommendations and selected news feeds.

These capabilities must be introduced progressively and justified by actual project needs.
